Stride v3.6.11

A free running and cycling app with a companion website. Every run turns into a 3D flyover: the camera starts above the map, tilts into real terrain and chases your line. Stride records it as a video you can post straight to your stories.

3D video
Satellite imagery draped on elevation tiles with MapLibre GL, recorded on the phone and shared as MP4, like the one beside this.
Record
Run, ride, walk and hike with a live 3D map, voice coach in English or 中文, auto-pause and saved routes.
Together
Group runs with live positions, group chats, cheers, friends by email or QR code.
Analyse
Splits with grade-adjusted pace, pace and heart-rate zones, effort score, best efforts from 400 m to 50 km.

Kira 记啦 v2.3.2

An ad-free money manager that mostly keeps itself. Pay with Touch 'n Go or your bank app and the payment is already waiting to be confirmed. Kira means "to count" in Malay; 记啦 means "logged it!"

Auto-log
Reads payment alerts from Malaysian bank apps, e-wallets and bank SMS on Android. Nothing is saved until you tap ✓.
Scan
Receipts and history screenshots read on the phone with ML Kit, in English, Malay and Chinese. Nothing is uploaded.
Plan
Budgets with rollover, recurring bills it spots by itself, credit-card statement cycles, installment plans.
Privacy
No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking. Works offline; syncs only to your own Google account.

    Chibi desktop companion

    A small character that lives on the Windows desktop and follows the mouse around. It slashes while you type, sends a ripple out when you click, and curls up to sleep when the computer goes quiet. It ships with a cursor set I drew to match.

    Reacts
    Slashes while you type, ripples on click, and after a long typing combo, swords fall from the sky.
    Sleeps
    Dozes off after a set number of idle seconds and wakes the moment you move.
    Polite
    Keeps clear of the cursor and taskbar, hides for fullscreen apps and screen sharing. About 30 MB of memory.
    Privacy
    It only notices that a key was pressed, never which key.

    Awake
